Canopy

Connect your X account

Canopy reads your DM inbox through the X API and only replies to people who already messaged you. Reads and sends are billed to your own X developer app.

Paste a user access token instead

Use this if you minted a token yourself. It needs dm.read, dm.write, tweet.read and users.read. An app-only Bearer Token won't work for DMs.

How replies should sound
Reach everyone, not just the last 30 days

X's API only returns DMs from the last 30 days. Your X data archive has all of them, so importing it brings back everyone who ever messaged you.

  1. On X, open Settings → Your account → Download an archive of your data and request it. X emails you when it's ready, usually within a day or two.
  2. Unzip it. In its data folder, find direct-messages.js. Big archives split it into direct-messages-part1.js and so on, so pick all of them.
  3. Choose the files below. They're read in this browser and not uploaded anywhere. Canopy then looks up each person's name and handle on X, which is billed to your X app.
These are sample conversations so you can see how it works. They can't be sent. Connect X and load your inbox to see the real people who messaged you.
