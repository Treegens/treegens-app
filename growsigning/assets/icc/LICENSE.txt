sRGB.icc

This colour profile was made by scripts/make-srgb-icc.mjs in this repository from the published numbers of the sRGB
colour space (IEC 61966-2-1: its primaries, its white point and its transfer curve). It contains no file and no data
taken from anyone else's profile, so no third-party licence applies to it.

Its authors place it in the public domain. Where that is not possible, anyone may use, copy, change and redistribute
it, for any purpose, with or without GrowSigning, with no conditions. It comes with no warranty.

It is the output intent of the PDF/A copies of completed documents (src/server/pdfa.ts).
