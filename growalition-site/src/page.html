The countries

Eight nations across the tropical belt

From the Amazon to the Pacific, coalition members hold some of the planet's most important forests, mangroves and reefs. Seven sit between the tropics. One plants in the mountains just north of them.

How the day works

From seedling to proof

Grow the stock

Each country raises native seedlings in local nurseries in the months before the day, matched to the soil, rainfall and coastline where they will go.

Map the sites

Governments and partners pick degraded land, river edges and coastlines where trees are wanted and will be looked after.

Plant together

On 5 June, communities, schools and volunteers plant across every member country in the same 24 hours.

Record and verify

Planters record geotagged evidence of their work with the Treegens app, so the count is public and survival can be checked over time.

Principles

A billion trees only counts if they live

Planting fast is easy. Planting well is the work. These rules apply in every member country.

Native only
Local species that belong in the landscape. No monoculture plantations counted toward the goal.
Protect first
Planting adds to standing forests. It never replaces protecting the ones we already have.
Local hands
Communities who live beside the trees plant them, care for them and share in the benefits.
Open counting
Every planting is recorded with location and evidence, and the totals are published.
Years, not days
The day is the start. Sites are revisited so survival is measured, not assumed.
