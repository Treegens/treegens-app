Keep half the logo's height clear on every side (about the height of its lower case letters). For the mark alone, half its width.

The logo is 6.05 times as wide as it is tall (290.48 by 48), so with its clear space it takes 7.05 by 2 of its heights: 22 px tall is 133 px wide, in a space 155 by 44. Stacked, 1.51 to 1. The stamp, 6.15 to 1.

Three things not to do
Do not stretch, squash or tilt it. The 45 degree lean is part of the drawing.
Do not put logo.svg on a dark ground: ink on deep green is 1.57 to 1. Use logo-dark.svg.
Grow Signer
Do not type the name next to the mark, or split it in two. Use the drawn logo.

Also: no outlines, shadows or gradients; do not swap the two halves' colours; nothing inside the clear space.

Voice, as the product already writes
  1. Plain words. Upload a PDF, add who needs to sign and place the fields. It takes about a minute.
  2. Say what happened and what to do next. We could not open this envelope. It may have been deleted, or it belongs to someone else on your team. Ask them to share it, or go back to your list.
  3. Never promise what the code does not do. Every dollar plants 10 mangrove trees with Treegens. (A fact from the price list, not "every signature saves a tree".)
  4. No em dashes. Commas, colons and full stops do the work. Signed digitally, so no paper, no printer and no courier.
  5. No jargon. Do not forward this email. The link is personal to you.
